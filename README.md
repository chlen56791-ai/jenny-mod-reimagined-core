# Minecraft Jenny Mod: The Interactive Companion Experience for All Versions

In the vast, blocky universe of Minecraft, players have always sought companionship beyond the occasional wolf or tamed parrot. The **Minecraft Jenny Mod** answers this call by introducing a fully interactive, lifelike NPC companion named Jenny who walks, talks, reacts, and evolves alongside your adventures. This isn’t just another mob reskin—it’s a deep, behavior-driven companion system that adapts to your playstyle, listens to your commands, and transforms the solo mining experience into a cooperative journey.

Think of Jenny as your in-game co-pilot—a digital companion who digs with you, fights beside you, and even engages in meaningful conversation through a custom dialogue engine. Whether you’re exploring ancient ruins, building a mountain fortress, or simply watching the sunset over your pixelated homestead, Jenny brings a layer of emotional depth and interactive realism that vanilla Minecraft simply cannot offer. Built for stability across every major version of the game, this mod ensures that no player is left behind, regardless of whether you’re on an older snapshot or the latest release.

---

## 📖 Overview: A Companion That Feels Alive

The core philosophy behind this mod is simple: **companionship should feel natural**. Jenny is not a static NPC with pre-written lines. Instead, she uses a robust **adaptive reaction system** that monitors your actions, environment, and biometric data (like health and hunger) to decide how she responds. Dig too deep without torches? She’ll warn you. Enter a dark cave alone? She’ll follow with a torch ready. Spend an hour building in creative mode? She’ll sit, relax, and comment on your architectural choices.

This is achieved through a **lightweight, event-driven AI framework** that runs entirely client-side, meaning you don’t need a server-side plugin or external database. The mod integrates seamlessly with the vanilla game loop, ensuring no performance drops even on lower-end machines. Every animation, from her idle breathing to her running gait, is custom-crafted to blend perfectly with Minecraft’s blocky aesthetic while adding a fluid, organic feel.

## 🎮 Unique Gameplay Mechanics

### The Trust System
Jenny’s effectiveness scales with your relationship. Start as strangers, and she’ll be hesitant to take on tough mobs. Spend time together, share food, and help her in battle to raise her trust level. At maximum trust, she gains a 15% damage boost and will even sacrifice her own health to save you from fatal falls.

### The Companion Wheel
A radial menu (similar to GTA’s weapon wheel) gives you quick access to commands: Follow, Stay, Attack, Guard, and Explore. You can also set specific behavioral toggles—like “avoid water” or “collect drops automatically”—to fine-tune her autonomy.

### Shared Experience Pool
While Jenny doesn’t level up independently, she benefits from a **shared experience system**. When you gain XP, she gains a portion of it, which enhances her movement speed, carrying capacity, and combat efficiency. This encourages you to play actively rather than AFK.

### Seasonal Events
During real-world holidays, Jenny’s dialogue and animations change. She’ll wear a tiny pumpkin hat during Halloween, a Santa cap in December, and a flower crown in spring. These seasonal touches add a delightful layer of immersion and replayability.

## 🔧 Configuration: Make It Your Own

Every player has a unique playstyle, and Jenny should adapt to yours. The mod’s configuration file exposes advanced settings, including:

- **Dialogue frequency** (from constant chatting to rare quips)
- **Aggression threshold** (how proactive she is in combat)
- **Follow distance** (from tight leash to loose wandering)
- **Day/night behavior** (she can sleep when you sleep or stay on guard)
- **Difficulty scaling** (adjust her stats for Hardcore mode)

The config file supports **hot-reloading**—edit it in a text editor while the game is running, and changes apply instantly without restarting your world.
